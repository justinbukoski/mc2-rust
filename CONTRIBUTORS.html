Contributors

MC2 Rust was directed by its owner (GitHub: justinbukoski) and written almost entirely by AI models, working as coders, reviewers and project managers. Every model that took part is credited here.

The counts come from the project's private development history as of 6 November 2026:

  • Commits: Co-Authored-By trailers on the development branch. A commit can carry more than one trailer.
  • Coding rounds: lane logs. A lane is one task, given to a coder and checked by a reviewer.
  • Reviews: review reports on disk, including superseded ones.

The public repository holds only a single exported commit, so these numbers can't be checked from it.
